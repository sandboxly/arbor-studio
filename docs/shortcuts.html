Keyboard and mouse

This page lists the mouse actions in the viewport and the keyboard shortcuts. Keys without a modifier, such as F and Esc, work while no text field is being edited.

Viewport

ActionMouse or trackpad
Orbit around the treeDrag with the left button (on a trackpad: click and drag)
PanDrag with the right or middle button
ZoomScroll wheel, two-finger scroll or pinch
Select a generatorClick the tree with Pick: Generators
Select a single branchClick the branch with Pick: Branches
Change the lightingClick Unlit, Morning, Noon, Evening or Night at the top left of the viewport

Sliders and number fields

ActionHow
Change a valueDrag the slider, or drag the number to its right
Type a valueClick the number, type the value and press Enter
Read what a setting doesHold the pointer over its name
Open the curve for a settingClick the curve button at the end of its row

A drag on a slider is one step in the undo history.
